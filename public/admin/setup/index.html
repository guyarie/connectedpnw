Editing connectedpnw.com

Three ways to change the site's text and blog. Each needs a one-time setup of a few minutes. Every change goes live within about two minutes and can be undone.

1. From Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and click Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Connected PNW website and paste this URL:
  3. Click Add, then Connect. Sign in with your Google account and click Allow.
  4. Start a new chat and try: "What does the FAQ say about pricing?" or "Add an event on October 3 called …".

If Claude does not use the connector, check that it is switched on in the chat's tools menu (the + or sliders icon next to the message box).

2. From ChatGPT

  1. Open the Connected PNW Editor GPT:
  2. The first time it needs to read or change something it will ask you to sign in. Choose Sign in with Google and click Allow.
  3. Talk to it the same way as Claude.

Prefer to use it in your regular ChatGPT chats? In ChatGPT go to Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced → Developer mode, then Create a connector with the URL from section 1.

3. The editing page

For seeing every field of a page at once, reordering events, or changing images: connectedpnw.com/admin. Sign in with your GitHub account.

Good to know

Undo. Ask "undo my last change" or "show recent changes and undo the one about …". Every change is kept forever, so nothing is lost.

Blog posts start as drafts. A new post is not visible until you say "publish it".

Is it live yet? Ask "is my change live?". Publishing takes about two minutes.

Images can't be uploaded through chat yet. Use the editing page, or send the image to the site's developer.
